# Project Overview - UrbanChain-VN

## Muc tieu MVP
UrbanChain-VN la prototype blockchain ho tro quy trinh dang ky dat dai va bien dong dat dai trong boi canh quan ly do thi.

## Pham vi MVP
1. Dinh danh va phan quyen
2. So hoa ho so va luu tru IPFS
3. Dang ky dat dai lan dau
4. Duyet / tu choi ho so
5. Tra cuu thong tin thua dat
6. Dang ky bien dong do chuyen nhuong
7. OCR + canh bao ho so
8. Dashboard / bao cao co ban

## Stack du kien
- Smart contract: Solidity + Hardhat
- Backend: Node.js + Express
- Frontend: React + TypeScript
- Storage: MySQL + IPFS
- Testing: Mocha/Chai + Cypress

## Co cau nhan su
- 3 PM con nguoi: Technical Lead, Schedule & Cost Lead, Quality & Risk Lead
- 15 AI Agents la nguon luc thuc thi

## Ranh gioi he thong
- Hien tai la prototype/demo hoc thuat
- Khong thay the quyet dinh hanh chinh thuc te
- Blockchain/IPFS chi la lop so hoa, truy vet va kiem chung bo tro
